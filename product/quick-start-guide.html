Manager Performance Tracker

Quick Start Guide · Setup takes about 5 minutes

1. Open the tracker

  1. Unzip the download. On Windows, right-click the zip and choose Extract All. On a Mac, double-click the zip.
  2. Move the Manager Performance Tracker folder somewhere permanent, such as Documents.
  3. Double-click Manager Performance Tracker.html. It opens in your web browser. There's nothing to install and you don't need an account.
  4. Bookmark the page (Ctrl+D on Windows, ⌘+D on a Mac) so you can find it again quickly.
Works on Windows and Mac computers in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari or Firefox. Use the same browser each time, because your data is saved in that browser.

2. Make it yours

  1. The tracker opens with sample data so you can explore. When you're ready, click Start Fresh on the Dashboard, or go to Settings → Start Fresh.
  2. In Settings, enter your name, store name and weekly goals. You can rename the four metrics to match what your company tracks.
  3. On the Team tab, click + Add Team Member for each person. Click any cell to edit it, and use × to remove a row.
  4. In Settings → Today's huddle, write your focus, team challenge and recognition for the day.

3. Your weekly routine

Every dayEnter yesterday's numbers on Daily Results. Each row is a real date, and today is highlighted. Update the huddle in Settings, then open the Huddle tab or click Print Huddle for your team meeting.
Through the weekLog coaching conversations on Coaching and update progress on Training. The Dashboard's What needs attention panel shows you where to focus.
End of weekGo to Settings → Download Backup. You don't need to do anything else: every Monday the tracker starts a new week by itself and saves last week's totals to the weekly trend. Your schedule carries over, so check it on the Schedule tab.

4. Keep your data safe

Everything you type is saved automatically inside your browser on this computer. Nothing is sent over the internet, so your team's information stays private.

Because of that, download a backup every week: Settings → Your data → Download Backup. This saves a small .json file to your Downloads folder. The Dashboard reminds you if it's been more than 7 days.

Your data can be lost if you: clear your browser history or "site data", use a private or incognito window, switch to a different browser, or move the tracker file in Firefox. If any of these happen, restore your latest backup.

5. Move to a new computer or browser

  1. On the old computer, go to Settings → Download Backup.
  2. Copy the tracker folder and the backup .json file to the new computer, for example with a USB drive or by email.
  3. Open the tracker on the new computer, then go to Settings → Restore Backup and choose the .json file.

Troubleshooting

My data is goneMake sure you're using the same browser as before and not a private window. If it's still missing, go to Settings → Restore Backup.
Phone or tabletThe tracker is made for desktop and laptop computers. Phones and tablets often can't open downloaded HTML files properly.
PrintingClick Print Huddle on the Huddle tab, or use your browser's Print command on any page. The menu and buttons are hidden automatically.
Start overGo to Settings → Start Fresh. This clears your team and results but keeps your goals. Load Sample Data brings back the demo.
