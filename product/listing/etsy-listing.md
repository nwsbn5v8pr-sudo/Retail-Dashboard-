## Description (copy and paste)
Run your store from one screen. The Manager Performance Tracker brings your sales numbers, your team, coaching, training, schedule and weekly report together in one easy tracker made for retail store managers.

No spreadsheets to set up, no formulas to break, no subscription and no account. Download it, double-click it, and it opens in your web browser.

★ WHAT'S INSIDE
• Dashboard – sales vs goal, conversion, average sale and customer experience at a glance, with comparisons to last week and a "What needs attention" list
• Daily Results – one row for each day of the week; a new week starts automatically and last week moves to your trend chart
• Team Scorecard – each associate's weekly target, sales, transactions and items sold, with average sale, items per sale, % of target and a leaderboard
• Team member profiles – coaching, training, goals, schedule and 1:1 notes for each person in one place
• Coaching, 1:1 Notes, Training and Goals trackers – with follow-up dates and overdue reminders
• Schedule – type shifts like 9-5 or 10:30-7 and the hours add up; see who's working today
• Daily Huddle – today's focus, team challenge and recognition, ready to print
• Weekly Report – a one-page summary for your district manager: print it, save it as a PDF, copy it into an email, or download it as a spreadsheet
• Spreadsheet export – open your data in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets

★ MAKE IT YOURS
• Rename the four store metrics to match what your company tracks
